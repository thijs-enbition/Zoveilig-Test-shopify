## Open items

1. **Do not publish unverified DPA claims.** Each processor's DPA must be verified and the evidence
   filed before the public page asserts it exists.
2. **The processor register must include product platforms, not only martech.** Securitas (meldkamer),
   NAMI, Climax, Alarm.com and Netlify (forms) all process personal data. Securitas is likely a
   separate or joint controller rather than a processor and needs documenting as such.
3. **Article 9.** Wellbeing monitoring and fall detection may constitute health data about vulnerable
   adults. A DPIA must be completed **before** processing begins, not retrospectively.
4. Required statutory disclosures still missing from drafts: controller identity and KvK number, the
   right to complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, actual retention periods, and the lawful
   basis per purpose.
5. Two processing activities are currently undisclosed: hashed identifiers sent to Google and Meta
   for Enhanced Conversions and Advanced Matching, and offline conversion uploads carrying the click
   identifier and contract value.

Dutch language drafts exist in the marketing OS repository under `clients/zo-veilig/website/policies/`.
Reconcile rather than duplicate. All of the above requires review by a qualified lawyer.
